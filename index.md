# SpeakCode Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

SpeakCode ("the game") is made by Adil Abazzar ("we"). This policy says what the game does with your data.

Contact: speakcode6@gmail.com

## The short version
- We run no servers and have no accounts. **We do not collect, store or sell your data.**
- Your voice is never recorded or saved.
- The game is played through Steam, so Valve's privacy policy also applies.

## Your voice
The game needs a microphone for two things:

1. **Voice commands.** Speech recognition (Vosk) runs on your own PC, offline. The audio is turned into words on your PC and thrown away. It is not sent to us or to any speech service.
2. **Proximity voice chat.** While you talk (push-to-talk or voice activity, your choice in Settings), your voice is compressed and sent to the other players in your lobby so that those near you in the game can hear you. It travels over Steam's networking, which may pass it through Valve's relay servers. Nobody's game saves it. Other players could still record what they hear with their own software, as in any voice chat.

You can mute your microphone, mute any player, or turn voice chat volume down in the game. Voice commands and voice chat stop when you close the game.

## Steam information
When you join a lobby, the other players see your Steam name and the game uses your Steam ID to connect you, to show who is who, and to let the host kick or ban a player from their lobby. This comes from Steam; we do not keep a copy.

Online play is peer-to-peer: one player hosts the match on their PC. Steam's networking normally hides your IP address from other players by using Valve's relays.

## Files the game saves on your PC
- **Settings** (graphics, audio, key bindings, microphone choice, character look). Stored on your PC only.
- **Match reports.** If you host a match, the game may write a short report of that match (mini-games played, commands used, player names, who won) to a folder on your own PC. It is not uploaded anywhere. You can delete these files at any time.

## What we do not do
- No advertising and no ad tracking.
- No analytics or tracking by us.
- No selling or sharing of personal data.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's hardware statistics are switched off, so the engine does not send device information either.

## Children
The game is not aimed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for a Steam account in your country). It has live voice chat with other players.

## Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. For the data Steam holds, see Valve's privacy policy: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/ . If you have a question, write to the contact address above.

## Changes
If the game starts handling data differently (for example crash reports or player reports), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
